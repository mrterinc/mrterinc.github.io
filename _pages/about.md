I am an Assistant Professor of Accounting at BI Norwegian Business School, Department of Accounting and Operations Management. I received my PhD from Bocconi University. My primary topics of interest are audit oversight, economics of audit markets, financial reporting misconduct and accounting of cryptoassets.

I also maintain a public tracker of the comment letters on the SEC's proposed Regulation Crypto Assets (File No. S7-2026-27), which you can find under <a href="/resources/"><strong>resources</strong></a>.
